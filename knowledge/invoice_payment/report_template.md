# Manager Report Template

A manager-facing invoice payment review report should be written in Markdown and include:

1. Case summary: case id, case type, current status, and what the user asked the agent to review.
2. Materials status: invoice, purchase order, goods receipt, vendor record, duplicate payment check, plus any process evidence such as BPI logs.
3. Evidence review: accepted, weak, rejected, conflicting, or process-only evidence, with short quoted snippets and source type.
4. Matching and gaps: what ties together, what is only partially supported, and what cannot yet be concluded.
5. Risk flags: duplicate risk, vendor mismatch, amount mismatch, missing GRN, weak provenance, process-order anomaly, or Clear Invoice boundary.
6. Next steps: specific materials or clarifications needed from the user.

The report must not claim that the workbench agent approved, paid, posted, routed, or submitted anything in ERP.
